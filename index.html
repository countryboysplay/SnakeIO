Noodle Piteat, grow, don't get bonked

Five rounds: grow to the target as the biggest worm in the pit, then face the Pit King. Use the joystick to steer. Hold the boost button to speed up (it costs length). Bump into a smaller worm to eat it. Touch a bigger one and it's over.

To install: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
